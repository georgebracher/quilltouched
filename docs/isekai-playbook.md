# EVERQUILL — Isekai Trope Playbook

*Produced 2026-07-12 by two research squads run in parallel and cross-checked: 6 Claude web-research agents (one lens each + a synthesis pass) and 4 GPT-5.6-sol researchers (xhigh reasoning, web search) on complementary lenses. All 11 raw reports preserved in `~/Downloads/Everquill Research/`. This doc is the merged DECISION document — verdicts only; evidence lives in the reports.*

---

## 1. The identity

**Tagline (use it everywhere, it tested out of the trope canon itself):**

> **The System records what you know — not what is true.**

**Spine principle:** every "game-like" institution in the world — guild ranks, church appraisals, monster grades, potion labels — is the world's own attempt to build KNOWN sheets, and all of them can be wrong. Players get the shapes anime taught them; the engine keeps the truth.

**The cheat skill:** isekai audiences expect the newcomer to have leverage. Everquill's is a three-layer stack, each layer a proven genre convention: Appraisal-in-world (Spider), the System window interface (Solo Leveling/DanMachi), and honest knowledge-across-deaths (Re:Zero minus the plot armor, Grimgar-grade loss). Pitch line: *"your cheat skill is that you're genre-savvy and you take notes."* **Intel is the cheat.**

**The Re:Zero inversion is the marketing pitch:** Re:Zero = he remembers, the world resets. Everquill = **the world remembers, you don't** (lossy journals, scarred world, public dice). Player-as-Subaru also dissolves the Subaru-hate problem — nobody resents their own retries.

**Death promise (Codex squad's phrasing, worth keeping verbatim):**

> The dice will not save you. The narrator will not save you. But the world will not erase you.

## 2. Cross-model verdicts (both squads independently agreed — highest confidence)

1. **Hide true numbers.** Not an isekai contract violation — a *marketable rejection of omniscient LitRPG*. Only ONE trope is truly mandatory (the crossing itself + consequential outsider perspective); status windows are "expected but replaceable with a deliberate substitute," and the Codex IS that substitute. Prestige cover: Mushoku Tensei, Re:Zero, Bookworm are all recognizably isekai with no HUD.
2. **Never meter the conversation; gate the world.** AI Dungeon's energy bar and Dungeon Keeper's core-verb timers are the sector's two most punished monetizations. Timers on slow projects only; dialogue, combat turns, inspection, and the immediate consequence of a typed action are NEVER gated.
3. **The market has voted for lethality.** Latitude's Wayfarer ("no plot armor") outperformed expectations and their Heroes product is trying to bolt mechanics onto an LLM adjudicator — the engine-referees-everything inversion remains unshipped at scale. Everquill starts where the leader is trying to arrive. Nobody anywhere has shipped an epistemic status screen.
4. **Component laws are canon-validated.** Log Horizon's Contract Art Ceremony (scholarship + rare materials + consent + narrow opportunity = a new rule of reality) is the exact model for sealed feats. The law is never negotiated by the narrator; every component obtainable by any route.
5. **Death restarts at the world's altitude, never at the tutorial.** Heir gets low power but mature-world engagement routes (investigate, negotiate, carry messages, exploit known routes). Memorials work through concrete facts, never generated praise. No death farming: dying never yields guaranteed power/currency/components.
6. **Isekai tropes sell; isekai branding doesn't.** Colopl's Isekai∞Isekai died in 15 months carrying the genre's three biggest IPs; loop quality wins. Spend on making the KNOWN-sheet/heir/intel loop *feel* like the genre, not on the word "isekai."
7. **Skips accelerate the whole world, honestly.** A paid skip advances every relevant clock (as waiting would) and previews which known deadlines move. It changes when, never whether. Both squads flagged compounding as the real pay-to-win risk (more cycles ≠ better odds but = more power) — audit every skip for second-order acceleration.

## 3. The big ruling: the two-layer Codex (Chronicle / Inquiry)

Resolves the hidden-numbers question with a sharper structure than "hide everything":

- **The Chronicle — authoritative.** Exact, immutable facts of record: time, coin, inventory, wounds, named abilities, public guild rank, and **the dice** — the 2d6 roll and outcome tier shown *after* resolution with known modifiers. The narrator cannot contradict it. This is what keeps lethality fair (XCOM lesson: players must be able to answer "why did I die?" without trusting prose).
- **The Inquiry — fallible.** Creature dossiers, motives, weaknesses, geography, history, threat reads. Every entry authored by a named source with provenance stamp, acquisition method, world-date, and scope. May be incomplete, outdated, or confidently wrong.

**Pre-roll: assessments in ladder words, never percentages.** Post-roll: full dice visibility. Precision is earned; provenance, not confidence bars ("here's where this came from," never "this might be wrong").

**Appraisal output = decision-useful observations, not stats:**

> **ASH-WOLF — Appraisal II**
> Threat: severe to an unarmored traveler
> Evidence: dragged a mailed guard six paces; tracks suggest an injured foreleg
> Claim: "Fire drives them off" — attributed to Warden Hessa
> Unresolved: whether the pack shares one den

**Wrong intel must be causal, never random.** Deterministic error mechanisms (reusable list, engine-owned): sample contamination, local-mistaken-for-universal, disguised subject, insufficient appraisal tier, corrupt official, transcription error, outdated info, deliberate counterintelligence, inherited note missing its qualifier. Repeating the same bad experiment under the same conditions reproduces the same misleading evidence.

**Claim schema** (both Codex squads converged on nearly identical fields — this is the Team A/F data model): subject · claim · claimed value/range · source · observer · acquisition method · world timestamp · scope · confidence(= evidence consistency, never truth-probability) · corroboration links · contradictions · inheritance status.

## 4. ADOPT / TWIST / AVOID (condensed master list)

### ADOPT — play straight (comfort furniture)
Adventurer's Guild with hall/board/F–S plates/registration ritual · full System UI grammar (`[brackets]`, `???`, toasts, chimes, `<<Proceed?>>` dialogs) · a unique entry blessing (= Codex-sight, see TWIST) · Appraisal-as-progression · reincarnation framing on the heir loop · **travel-in-days-by-carriage** (world-time gating is genre-native map scale) · monster ladder slime→dragon with letter grades · copper/silver/gold coins · tavern as rumor hub · found-party of flawed NPC specialists · Bookworm-style research-is-the-power-fantasy track · potion furniture · nobles-vs-commoners friction · demihumans rare and regional · info brokers & horde events · System speaks terse machine-English, no honorifics · flat-color LN sprite style ~5 heads · short brand + LN-style long subtitle for marketing.

### TWIST — the architecture IS the subversion (say it in marketing)
- **Status screen → a status screen that can lie** (flagship mechanic; provenance stamps as System chrome).
- **Level-up → knowledge is the level-up**: the re-tier toast IS the ding — `[Reappraised: Hollow King — HOPELESS → DESPERATE]`, full chime.
- **Return by Death → inverted and honest**: world remembers, heir forgets; unjournaled knowledge genuinely dies — a cost Re:Zero never charges.
- **Cheat skill → Codex-sight as the soul-bound blessing** (natives can't see the System; the blessing follows the soul = heir continuity; entry scene = local "Quill-touched" awakening in god's-waiting-room dress).
- **Skill Church**: public appraisal ceremony writes a *fallible public KNOWN entry* on you; "the church misjudged me" = trash-skill fantasy with epistemic teeth.
- **Hero summoning → historical sealed-tier event**; player is local, living in the wreckage; the same laws that permitted summoning gate resurrection (one authored answer serves entry lore + sealed tier + heir continuity).
- **Demon Lord → flagship pinboard hypothesis** ("real, dead, committee, or propaganda?" — church's answer and truth authored separately).
- **Guild rank = the guild's KNOWN data**, deliberately fuzzy vs engine tiers; rank-mismatch disaster is canon drama.
- **DanMachi falna → deed log as level-up currency**: witnessed feats mint titles (`[Title Earned: Ratcatcher of Dunmere]`) that propagate by rumor and gate NPC treatment — XP replacement with full genre legitimacy.
- **Konosuba deflation → impossible-tier mockery via System chrome**, engine-budgeted so comedy never curdles the drama.
- **Player genre literacy → trope-bait**: world holds ~70% of isekai defaults; deviations feed the wrong-intel system ("trolls regen unless burned… here they don't" — Shield Hero's failed heroes, made playable).
- **Overspecialization joy (Bofuri)** → component-law combos the engine honors: lawful-but-unintended combinations left in deliberately.

### AVOID — radioactive or spent
Slavery in any framing (steepest documented backlash + storefront risk) · harem/affection meters · effortless OP arrival · VRMMO/trapped-in-game framing (invites "log out"; the System is not software) · **true numbers anywhere, including your own sheet** · truck-kun beyond one dry deniable nod · sycophantic NPC awe the engine didn't earn (Realist Hero failure — standing narrator-prompt QA rule) · blue-box spam in prose (boxes live in Codex/toasts; prose stays prose) · **metering the conversation** · meta-parody as identity (must be playable by someone who's never seen an isekai) · underage-coded companions · isekai IP-cosplay · Earth-food beats (breaks locked universe — cuisine is regional intel instead) · casual goblin-genocide-as-chores (deed log remembers) · chibi or 7-head realism sprites.

## 5. Systems promoted from the LN deep-cuts (Codex squad 1)

Adopted into design (roadmap-relevant):
1. **Innovation = supply-chain quest (Bookworm).** Major feats resolve against hidden dependency graphs: concept → tested procedure → specialist → tooling → input quality → facility → legal access → production time. 7–9 rolls yield "correct principle, omitted dependency."
2. **Guilds are regulators, not quest hubs.** Settlements get a regulatory map (who may trade, who certifies, what's monopolized, who adjudicates). A superior product doesn't automatically win — licensing/concealing/bribing/rival-guild are all lawful routes.
3. **Contracts compile.** Player phrases the deal freely; the engine compiles it into visible clauses (parties, scope, duration, enforcement, termination) before signature. Never narrator-adjudicated.
4. **Retainer loyalty is a vector, not a scalar (Overlord):** hidden TRUE fields — loyalty target, doctrine, taboo, ambition, interpretation style. KNOWN says "utterly loyal." Heirs inherit office, not affection. Succession drama without arbitrary betrayal.
5. **Relics are irreplaceable capital:** provenance, finite count, incomplete identification (effect known, charges unknown), destructive testing, depleted exactly as the predecessor left them. Never refilled by dailies.
6. **Pressure fronts (Shield Hero waves):** scheduled world-time disasters with visible countdowns and incomplete intel; overlapping clocks so the player can't solve everything; failure leaves permanent state the heir inherits. Skips preview which fronts advance.
7. **Appraisal tiers reveal FIELDS, not accuracy %** (Kumo): surface description → category → bands → ranged estimate + provenance → modifiers/motives. Some targets notice, some feed false signals.
8. **Techniques are knowledge-gated (Kumo):** no universal skill tree; you can't attempt what you don't know exists. Libraries/teachers/journals control the possibility space. KNOWN can include nonexistent techniques.
9. **Classes emerge from behavior (Wandering Inn):** no class picker at chargen — hidden practice vectors from resolved actions; at thresholds the System offers recognition (Envoy, Pathfinder, Whisper-Regent). Build = autobiography. *(Changes chargen: rolls set aptitudes; identity emerges.)*
10. **Forbidden-knowledge exposure track (Kumo Taboo):** researching sealed domains (resurrection law, divine anatomy) accrues exposure — more TRUE access, more surveillance/nightmares/faction interest. Skips never bypass exposure.
11. **Dynastic estate (Overlord's Nazarick) = the cross-heir progression layer:** archives, workshops, retainers, charters, relics, liabilities; raidable and confiscatable, never an invulnerable lobby. **Post-alpha direction, not MVP.**
12. **Living dungeons as stateful factions** (dungeon-core genre): hunger, temperament, spawn budget; raid/feed/negotiate/settle-near all valid. **Post-alpha direction.**

## 6. Death & the heir flow (Codex squad 3 — adopt nearly wholesale)

**Core thesis:** not a game about replacing failed characters — a game about **one world accumulating the cost of many singular lives**. Spiral, not loop.

**Principles:** death possible, not routine (injury/capture/retreat/disgrace do most failure work — overlethality teaches players attachment is irrational) · risk labels calculated by the engine, shown pre-roll: **Guarded / Perilous / Dire / Desperate / Suicidal** (merge with ladder vocabulary) · every death auditable from the Chronicle · never erase playtime — convert it into world history · heirs are continuations of consequences, not builds · mourning expressive but mechanically light (no grief taxes, no heir debuffs) · specific facts outperform generated sentiment ("Ilyra Fen, 23. Died at Glass Ford holding the western stair. Body unrecovered." beats any eulogy) · Grimgar texture: attachment via ordinary life (the shopkeeper stops making their usual order) · append-only canon (savescumming structurally ineffective).

**The heir flow (6 phases, target <90 seconds death → first meaningful heir decision):**
1. **Sealed death** — engine commits the full death record (cause, witnesses, body/journal disposition, rumor seeds) before narration; most deaths confused, abrupt, unfinished — no automatic eloquent speeches.
2. **Silence + factual death card** — Mourn now / See what the world did next / Choose the heir.
3. **World pulse** — advance only justified time; show only what the heir could know.
4. **Three candidates** — kin (access: letters, legitimacy; burden: inherited enemies) / apprentice (methods, contacts; burden: comparison) / finder-stranger (physical evidence; burden: no trust). *(Independently validated our exact three.)* No rerolling; declined candidates persist as NPCs.
5. **Stance toward the dead** — complete / avenge / understand / protect / clear the name / reject / exploit. Nonbinding orientation.
6. **Enter through an event, not a menu** — the death notice, the distorted tavern rumor, the corpse.

**Journal capture must be low-friction:** after significant scenes the engine proposes 2–5 mechanically-grounded facts, player picks which the character records (+optional personal note, typed or voice). Documentation as deliberate practice, never clerical labor. Journals are world objects (carried/cached/sent/stolen/burned by deterministic rules); destruction always telegraphed.

**Resurrection (five laws, inspectable):** identity · memory · vessel · passage · power. Known early, achieved endgame. **Never rewinds:** the heir's deeds stay real, the resurrected returns displaced by elapsed history — a difficult reunion, not a loaded save. The resurrected doesn't auto-replace the heir.

**Alpha validation criteria (add to Sprint 6 gates):** ≥90% of testers can explain the mechanical cause of their death · median death→first-heir-decision <90s · heirs described as different people · memorials revisited voluntarily · most-remembered deaths include modest/accidental ones.

## 7. Timer & skip economy (Codex squad 4 — the evidence-backed contract)

**The contract:** slow fictional work takes real time; it always completes safely offline; paying changes only *when* the result arrives; core conversation and dramatic play remain available; no player is punished for living on a once-daily schedule.

**Rules adopted (condensed from 11):**
1. Gate slow projects, never the conversational heartbeat (Genshin's story/farming separation is the safe precedent).
2. **Timers land after a payoff, not before one** — session ends after the player commissions the blade, never on "come back tomorrow" ransom (Hogwarts Mystery failure).
3. **Preview duration before committing** — "a cursory search tonight; a full concordance takes eight hours." Never reinterpret an innocent verb into an unseen 24h obligation.
4. **Design for one daily visit:** bands ~2h / 8h / 20–24h / 48–72h; nothing decays, expires, or needs a claim tap (Genshin's 16-hour cap is the schedule-coercion lesson).
5. Completion is automatic, offline, permanent; one optional push.
6. A long project never freezes the fantasy — other characters, local scenes, Codex work always available (Dungeon Keeper's ASA ruling was about *no meaningful action*, not duration).
7. Show fictional progress ("cataloguing → cross-referencing → translation"), not just digits.
8. Paid time never creates better outcomes OR compounding competitive power; keep the consequential game solo; no scarce first-arrival rewards.
9. **One transparent conversion** — show "removes 8h — $X equivalent" at purchase; no currency-within-currency (FTC's Genshin complaint).
10. Free skips are part of the trust contract: one meaningful skip at introduction, modest earnable supply after (AFK Arena's free first daily). Mastered routes become cheap/free sweeps (a road you've survived).
11. **Charge only on durable successful delivery; auto-recredit failures** — a paid-for bad LLM response converts model disappointment into monetary grievance.

**Provisional price bands** (declining per-hour curve on remaining time; last 10–15 min free): ≤2h $0.49–0.69 · ≤8h $1.29–1.79 · ≤24h $2.49–3.49 · ≤72h $4.99–5.99. Packs $1.99/$4.99/$9.99; **no whale packs at launch**; never show a discount while the player is blocked.

**Compute-cost floor:** list price ≥ K/((1−s)(1−m)) where K = p95 incremental LLM cost the skip triggers, s = store fee, m = margin target. At 30% fee + 60% margin → **≥3.57×K**. If the floor exceeds the market band, don't sell the skip — cut inference cost or leave that timer non-skippable with advance disclosure.

**Top backfire modes to watch in alpha:** (1) "the chatbot charges me to answer" — typed input makes artificial delay unusually visible; introduce world-time via an optional rewarding project before it ever blocks; (2) compounding acceleration read as pay-to-win by gacha-literate players; (3) paying to accelerate a disappointing generation.
