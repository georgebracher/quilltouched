# 07 — Summoning History

Scope: hero summoning as historical sealed-tier events — the wreckage the player lives in; the same component laws that permitted summoning gate resurrection; entry lore for the Quill-touched awakening (playbook §4 TWIST, roadmap §3.7). **Filled to universe depth (Universe Canon v1, 2026-07-14) — this file is no longer a stub.** Registered as `law.summoning` in `content/canon/cosmology-powers.yaml`. TRUE-sheet halves below are engine/authoring only and never reach narrator context.

## The premise, stated plainly

You are not the first otherworld soul pulled into this world. You are the latest. The great summonings already happened; their heroes are legend and their wreckage is the map you live on. This is the isekai twist the world runs on (playbook §4): players expect to *be* the summoned hero arriving to save the day — in Everquill you are local-born to the aftermath, a Quill-touched soul waking into a world that has done this before and paid for it.

## What everyone knows (KNOWN — narrator-safe)

Common sense across the kingdom, denomination-inflected but not disputed:

- **The summonings happened.** Generations back, in the era the chronicles call the Calling, otherworld heroes were pulled across to fight a calamity the songs no longer agree on (the Demon Lord, `08-demon-lord-dossier.md`; a great dragon, `content/canon/bestiary.yaml`; something worse). Some songs say it worked. All songs agree the price was ruinous.
- **The heroes were state assets.** They were summoned by crowns and colleges, bound to purposes, and used. What happened to them after is the part the ballads get quiet about — used up, discarded, turned on, or simply outlived by the world that summoned them.
- **The wreckage is still here.** The summoning-era left ruins the vale avoids: broken war-colleges, sealed sites, and things that should not still move (`content/canon/bestiary.yaml`, `bestiary.construct` — most extant constructs are inherited wreckage, not new work). The Redcliff quarry closing to a wyvern is the vale's small local version of "a place people used to work and now do not go."
- **The crown keeps the annals.** The deep records — the Summoning Annals (`content/canon/texts-knowledge.yaml`, `text.summoning_annals`) — are controlled by the crown and the monastery record-halls, because the answers implicate the people keeping them. What a parish knows is ballad; what the seat knows is guarded.
- **The Quill-touched.** Doctrine holds that one who sees the System bears the prophet's mark (`06-quill-cosmology.md`) — read *to*, where everyone else is only written *about*. The churches disagree ferociously about what that means. Neither doubts the mark is real.

## The sealed law (TRUE — engine and authoring only, never narrator context)

**Summoning and resurrection are two operations of one veil-crossing physic.** Resurrection returns a *known dead* across a thin place (`02-universe-laws.md`, `law.resurrection`). Summoning pulls an *unknown living soul* across from beyond. Both pay passage and power; both are sealed feats; the scholarship that cracked one is the scholarship that cracks the other. This is why the same component laws that permitted summoning gate resurrection (roadmap §3.7), and why a resurrection researcher like Elam Ferrier is standing near a well far deeper than his wife's grave.

`law.summoning`, modeled parallel to `law.resurrection`, five components:

- **passage** — a thin place, opened in the summoning direction, during its window. Thin places are thin to more than death; the summoning-era scholars *used* that fact. Whether the veil's far side and the place summoned souls come *from* are the same "beyond" is a bottomless question (see below) — the law does not need to answer it to work.
- **anchor** — something on this side for the pulled soul to land in and hold to (a vessel, a bound name, a made body). Without it the crossing tears.
- **caller** — an authority with the standing to reach across and the will to hold the line open. Crowns and colleges held this; it is not a thing one hedge-witch does alone.
- **purpose** — the summoning binds the pulled soul to a task. The binding is where the exploitation lives: the hero is summoned *for* something, and the something is not theirs.
- **power** — paid in lives, in the same coin as resurrection. The Calling's ruin is the size of this bill.

**Costs (engine-enforced when a summoning resolves):** the offered lives; the caller's standing spent; the thin window closed; forbidden-knowledge exposure at the deepest tier (`content/canon/cosmology-powers.yaml`, `power.forbidden_exposure`); and attention — the far side, and whatever else watches the crossings, now knows a door was opened and by whom.

## The player's awakening (TRUE — engine and authoring only; FLAG for George)

The Quill-touched player is a soul that sees the System, waking into the world. The *shape* is authored; the *deepest specifics* are George's to set, because they touch the Quill (bottomless) and the Demon Lord (`08`).

- Authored: the awakening is a summoning-adjacent event — the player arrives *touched*, able to read the [brackets] and the `<<dialogs>>` that natives cannot. The heir loop is reincarnation in the world's own language: "the Quill writes you again" (roadmap §3.7); the sight follows the soul, which is why heirs keep it.
- **FLAG (George's call):** who calls the current arrivals, and why the Calling never fully stopped. The honest, bottomless answer is that every revelation opens a deeper one — do not author a floor the game can hit. Candidate framings, none locked: the crown still calls, quietly, and lies about it; the Quill itself is the caller (which the game NEVER confirms — `06`); the crossings are self-opening now that so many have been forced. Keep the true half out of narrator context and keep the last question unanswerable.

## Binding canon notes

- **No effortless OP arrival** (`12-explicit-absences.md`). The summoning gift is the Codex sight, not combat power; the world is no easier for it. The wreckage of prior heroes sets the no-plot-armor tone — legendary summoned heroes have deed logs, and some of those logs end badly.
- **No VRMMO framing.** The summoning is a metaphysical crossing, not a login. There is no logging out and no respawn; death routes to the heir (`12`).
- **Truck-kun:** one dry, deniable nod ever, never a second (`12`). The Calling is a real historical atrocity, not a punchline.
- **Bottomless.** The summoning-lore research domain (`content/canon/texts-knowledge.yaml`, `knowledge.summoning_lore`) never bottoms out: tier 4 is "the reason it never stopped," and that answer opens the next. The crown's control of the annals is a KNOWN-sheet fact; the truth beneath it is deeper than the crown itself knows.
