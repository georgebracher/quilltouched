# 06 — The Quill: Cosmology & the Two Churches

Scope: the believed god, the two churches that dispute it, and the hard rule that the dispute never resolves (roadmap §3.12). **Written in full (B0.3) — this file is not a stub.**

## HARD RULE — the mystery is BOTTOMLESS (DECIDED, George 2026-07-13)

- **No end state, ever.** There is no "world at peace and you are god." Defeat the biggest threat and the victory itself uncovers the next development, with new issues and struggles.
- **Every revelation is real, and every revelation opens a deeper question.** Revelations on the Quill are never fake-outs — they are true, and they deepen the mystery rather than closing it.
- **The adjudicator never closes "what is the Quill."** The claim "prove what the Quill is" is permanently unsatisfiable. Component laws may gate *revelations*; they may never gate — or grant — the final answer.
- **The game never confirms the answer.** Not in prose, not in System chrome, not in a precedent ruling. The forbidden-knowledge exposure track on the Quill never terminates.

## The Quill, as believed

The Quill is the world's believed god: the instrument that wrote the world into being. The game's real metaphysics ARE the theological dispute. Both churches read the same true evidence, and both are half right — the dice are honest, so no story is pre-written; but the world *was* authored. Neither side can ever prove itself out, because the question is bottomless.

## The Open Page (pro-Quill orthodoxy — working name)

**Doctrine: the Page is open, and you hold the pen.** The Quill wrote the world; it does not write your lines. What you do is written *because* you did it — record is reverence.

- Runs the Skill Church ceremonies (see `05-skill-church.md`): public appraisal is a sacrament, and its verdict — fallible as any scribe — is entered on the parish rolls as a public KNOWN entry.
- Reads `<<Proceed?>>` as the Quill asking consent: their proof of free will. A god that compels does not ask. Nothing that asks may compel.
- **On the Quill-touched:** they bear the *prophet's mark*. To see the System is to glimpse the Page itself — such people are read to, not merely written about. Parishes seek them out; some bishops fear what they might read.
- **Death rite:** the deeds of the dead are read aloud from the rolls, and the rite closes: **"He wrote his last line himself."** A well-kept journal is a devotional object; dying unrecorded is the quiet tragedy.

## The Blotted (anti-Quill — working name)

**Doctrine: the Author writes us into corners and calls it a story.** Everything recorded is a bar on the cage. The only free act is the one no one wrote down.

- They seek to live **unwritten**: refuse the ceremonies, distrust records, keep no ledgers, sign nothing, dwell in the margins — border camps, drowned quarters, roads between parishes.
- **The cost is real and mechanical:** their unrecorded knowledge dies with each generation. No journals, no archives, no inheritance of what they learned — every Blotted elder takes a library into the grave, and they call that victory.
- **On the Quill-touched:** they are marked by *the Author's finger* — characters the story is currently touching. Pitied, feared, sometimes followed at a distance to see what the plot does next; never trusted with a secret worth keeping.
- **Death rite:** the dead are buried nameless, graves unmarked, possessions burned unread, and the rite closes: **"The Quill wrote him into a corner."** To record the death would be to finish the Author's sentence for him.

## Frictions and play surface (design notes)

- NPC reactions to the Quill-touched are rumor-driven and denomination-inflected: the same deed reads as prophecy to one parish and as plot to the next.
- System chrome doubles as scripture: impossible-tier mockery is cited by both churches — the Open Page as the Quill's honest margin-notes, the Blotted as the Author sneering at his characters.
- Both churches are fallible KNOWN-sheet builders (per `00-spine.md`): parish rolls can be wrong; Blotted oral lore decays deterministically.
