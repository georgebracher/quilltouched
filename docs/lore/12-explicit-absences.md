# 12 — Explicit Absences

Scope: what this world does not contain, ever. Binding on all teams — worldgen, narrator prompts, adjudicator rulings, and art. If generated content trips one of these, it is a validation failure, not a style note. **Complete (B0.1) — this file is not a stub.**

## Hard absences (never, in any framing)

- **No slavery content.** Not as villainy to defeat, not as backstory, not as economy flavor.
- **No harems.** No affection meters, no romance-collection mechanics.
- **No VRMMO logic.** The System is not software. Nobody can "log out." No patch notes, no respawns, no menus-as-reality. Death is death; the world is the world.
- **No Earth-food beats.** No character invents mayonnaise. Cuisine is regional intel in the locked universe, never a nostalgia import.
- **No truthful floating windows.** No window, box, or toast ever shows true numbers — including your own sheet. The Chronicle shows facts of record; the Inquiry can lie; true sheets exist only inside the engine.
- **No underage-coded companions.**

## Also out (playbook §4 AVOID, for completeness)

- No effortless OP arrival.
- Truck-kun: one dry, deniable nod ever; never a second.
- No sycophantic NPC awe the deed log didn't earn.
- No blue-box spam in prose (boxes live in Codex/toasts — see `01-system-diction.md`).
- No metering the conversation (timers gate slow projects only).
- No meta-parody as identity — the game must be playable by someone who has never seen an isekai.
- No isekai IP cosplay.
- No casual goblin-genocide-as-chores: the deed log remembers every routine kill.
- Sprites: no chibi, no 7-head realism (style bible owns enforcement — Team E).
